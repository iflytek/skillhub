# Social & Interaction

SkillHub provides lightweight interaction signals that help teams judge which skills are useful, trusted, and worth reusing.

## Core Capabilities

- Starring
- Ratings
- Download counts
- Notifications

## Common Uses

### Save Frequently Used Skills

- Star a skill on the detail page
- Return to it later from your personal starred list

### Leave a Rating

- Score a skill from 1 to 5
- Use ratings to help others judge quality and stability

### Review Notifications

- Review results
- Governance actions that affect your content
- Other in-product reminders that need attention

## How to Read These Signals

- High stars usually mean high reuse
- High ratings usually mean a better experience
- High downloads usually mean broader adoption

These signals are useful, but they should not replace human judgment.
