# Sample Plan - 40-person internal demo day, own office space

Worked example. Numbers are illustrative; the drivers are the point.

---

## 1. Plan Brief

```
Event:        Internal demo day - six teams show one thing each, then open floor
When:         2026-05-14 (Thu) 14:00-18:00 UTC+8  |  decision date: 2026-04-30  ->  14 days lead
Who:          40 internal staff  |  confidence: +/- 8  |  no external guests
Where:        own office, main floor + break area  |  setup window: 2 h  [verify] with facilities
Format:       standing reception with a seated talk block  |  duration 4 h
Budget basis: CNY 12,000 covering food, drinks, materials, AV rental
Success:      every team demos once, and 30+ people stay past the last slot
Biggest risk: AV - six laptops, one projector, no spare adapter set
Status:       PLAN ONLY - nothing sent, booked, quoted or licensed
```

ASSUMED: no alcohol (internal, weekday afternoon); no external catering contract, food ordered
from two nearby suppliers; facilities provides tables and power; one internal owner covers two
roles (AV + content), which is named below as a risk rather than hidden.

## 5. Budget

| Drinks, non-alcoholic | 700 | 1,100 | 1.5-2.0 x headcount over 4h | medium |
| Ice, cups, napkins | 250 | 400 | per head | low |
| Materials and print | 350 | 600 | per item, express excluded | low, slips cost express fees |
| AV rental (mics) + spares | 400 | 900 | per item x one day | high - day-rate extension |
| Transport / delivery | 200 | 350 | per trip x 2 suppliers | medium |
| Contingency | 900 | 1,300 | 10-15% of subtotal | this is the buffer, not a line to spend |

Range total: CNY 7,000 - 10,250 against a 12,000 cap, so the plan holds one extra supplier trip
inside the cap.

Known misses (not included above): service charge if a supplier adds one `[verify]`, venue
overtime after the contracted window, damage deposit, staff water for the two-hour setup, and
express print cost if the timeline slips past T-3.

Most likely overrun: food, driven by headcount - the headcount low/high band is what keeps this
honest. One tranche of contingency covers it.

## 7. Open Questions

1. Are external guests attending? Default: no. If yes, signage and check-in double.
2. Any dietary requirements already known? Default: 6 labelled variants, no assumption of who.
3. Can the room be held past 18:00? Default: no, teardown ends at 18:30.
4. Is recording required? Default: no. If yes, AV becomes a two-person role.
