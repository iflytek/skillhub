# Quantities and Materials

Every quantity has a **driver**. A number without a driver cannot be re-derived when the headcount
moves - and the headcount always moves.

## Drivers

| Item | Driver | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seats | 1.0 x headcount seated; 0.6 x standing; +10% for late arrivals | venue stock `[verify]` |
| Tables | 1 per 6-8 seated; 1 per 3 standing (cocktail) | round vs long changes the count |
| Drinks, non-alcoholic | 1.5-2.0 x headcount over 3h | more in summer or after activity |
| Drinks, alcoholic (only if confirmed) | 2.0-3.0 x headcount over 3h | **never assume**; ask |
| Ice | 0.5 kg per person | universally underestimated |
| Food, standing reception | 6-8 bites per person per hour | people eat more than planners expect |
| Food, seated | 1 portion per person + 5% spare | dietary variants are separate lines |
| Name tags | 1.1 x headcount | pens: 1 per 5 |
| Signage | 1 per entrance + 1 per stair + 1 per direction change | wayfinding failures look like chaos |
| Power outlets / strips | 1 per AV device + 2 spare | count devices, not people |
| Extension cords | 1 per 4m of distance from the outlet | measure, do not guess |
| Waste bins | 1 per 25 people + 1 per food station | full bins change the room's feel |
| Staff (service) | 1 per 15 seated / 1 per 25 standing | vendor will quote their own ratio `[verify]` |
| Toilets | existing venue stock, check ratio `[verify]` | a real capacity question, not ours to rule |
| Chairs for a talk | 0.9 x headcount | standing room absorbs the rest |
| Microphones | 1 per speaker + 1 spare | the spare is the whole point |
| Water for speakers | 1 bottle per speaker per block | |

## Materials checklist by zone

**Entrance / door**
- check-in list (printed, offline), name tags, pens, signage, a person who owns the door.
- a place to leave coats and bags; a bin for the tag backing.

**Room**
- seating per format, sightlines checked from the back row, aisle widths left clear `[verify]`.
- AV: laptop, adapter set, HDMI/USB-C, clicker, spare batteries, speaker timer visible to speaker.
- sound check with the actual room empty **and** with 20 people in it (bodies absorb sound).

**Food and drink**
- station placement away from the entrance and away from AV cables.
- labelled dietary variants; serving utensils; napkins; bins next to stations.
- water access that does not queue behind the bar.

**Exit / teardown**
- a named person for teardown, a checklist of what belongs to whom, a box for lost property.
- venue walkthrough with the contact before leaving, damage noted with photos `[verify]`.

## Three constraints that cause re-planning

1. **Power** - how many outlets, where, and what load. Ask before the layout is drawn.
2. **Surface** - which items need a table or a wall. Signage without a wall becomes a floor sign.
3. **Access** - delivery times, lift size, stair width, door width. A pallet that cannot pass the
   door is a same-day crisis.

## Rounding rules

- Round **up** for anything consumable, printed, or rented per unit.
- Add **5-10% spare** for printed items, name tags and glassware.
- Never round up a staffing line to save money - understaffing shows immediately.
- State the driver even when the number is obvious: `Ice | 20 kg | 0.5 kg x 40 people`.
