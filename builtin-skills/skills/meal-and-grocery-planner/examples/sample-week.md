## 2. Menu

| Day / Meal | Dish | Serves | Time | Equipment | Reuses | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon dinner | Tray chicken with lemon and herbs | 2 + 1 lunch | 40 min | oven + 1 tray | half the herb bunch, 1 lemon | hands-off, one tray, lunch built in |
| Tue dinner | Lentil and tomato pot with yoghurt | 2 | 30 min | pot (burner 1) | rest of the herb bunch, yoghurt | no oven needed, forgiving |
| Wed dinner | Egg and vegetable fried rice | 2 | 20 min | pan (burner 1) | leftover rice from Mon, half the spring onions | fastest night, uses Monday's rice |
| Thu dinner | Pan fish with green sauce | 2 | 25 min | pan (burner 2) | yoghurt, remaining spring onions | protein switch, 8 min cooking |
| Fri dinner | Chickpea and cucumber salad with bread | 2 + 1 lunch | 15 min | board only | lemon, herbs, bread from Thu | no cooking, absorbs leftovers |

Equipment check: no two dishes claim the same slot on the same night. The oven is used only on
Monday; burners never exceed two. Wednesday deliberately depends on Monday's rice.

## 7. Substitutions (no limit was stated)

| Dish | Contains | Vegetarian swap | Dairy-free swap | Gluten-free swap | Cost of the swap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tray chicken | - | firm tofu, pressed, same tray | - | - | 10 min extra, needs more salt |
| Lentil pot | dairy | already vegetarian | coconut yoghurt | - | thinner sauce |
| Fried rice | egg, soy | extra tofu, skip egg | skip egg | tamari | less binding, add sesame |
| Pan fish | fish | king oyster mushroom | - | - | texture changes most here |
| Chickpea salad | gluten (bread), dairy | already vegetarian | skip yoghurt | rice or GF bread | loses the scooping vehicle |

Read labels on soy sauce, stock and bread for hidden gluten and dairy `[verify]`. Shared pans and
boards matter as much as ingredients when someone avoids an allergen.
