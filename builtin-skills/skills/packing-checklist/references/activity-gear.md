# Activity Gear Matrix

Every activity adds two things: **footwear** and **one piece of gear**. Footwear is the real
volume problem - one extra pair of shoes costs more space than everything else combined.

| Activity | Adds footwear | Adds gear | Volume trade to name |
|---|---|---|---|
| City sightseeing | 1 walking shoe (worn) | day bag, water bottle | none - baseline |
| Business / formal | 1 dress shoe | 1 formal outfit, laptop | dress shoe replaces a second casual pair |
| Hiking day trip | 1 trail shoe or boot | rain shell, blister kit, headlamp, 20L pack | boots worn on travel days, not packed |
| Multi-day trek | 1 boot (worn) | 40-50L pack, layers, filter, sleep liner | checked bag becomes impossible for some routes `[verify]` |
| Beach / pool | 1 sandal | swimwear, quick-dry towel, sun hat | towel is the volume thief; use a hotel one |
| Winter sport | 1 boot (often rented) | goggles, gloves x2, base layers x2 | rent bulky items; only base layers travel |
| Cycling | 1 stiff sole shoe | helmet (often rented), repair kit, light | helmet volume vs rental availability `[verify]` |
| Photography / video | none | body + 1-2 lenses, batteries, cards, tripod decision | tripod is the single biggest optional item |
| Formal event / wedding | 1 dress shoe | outfit, accessories, gift space | gift return space is often forgotten |
| Camping | 1 boot | shelter, sleep system, stove | almost always checked or rented |
| Water / boat | 1 grippy sandal | dry bag, dry top | dry bags take volume even empty |
| Religious site visits | 1 slip-on (easy removal) | shoulder cover, scarf | often stated by the venue `[verify]` |

## Combination rules

- **Two footwear maximum** for carry-on: one worn, one packed. If a third is required, say which
  activity loses out.
- **One pair of shoes can serve two activities** if the surfaces are similar (city + light trail).
  Say when you are merging and what the merging costs.
- **Gear that can be rented** should not travel: helmet, boots, tent, tripod for a single use.
  Mark `rental `[verify]`` instead of adding a row.
- **Electronics**: one charger per device family, one power strip only if 3+ devices. Name the
  plug-type adapter if the destination differs `[verify]`.
- **Photography**: decide lens count explicitly. "Body + one lens" is a decision; "camera stuff"
  is not.

## Gear that is over-packed most often

| Item | Why it is packed | Cheaper alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Third pair of shoes | "in case" | one pair that covers both |
| Full-size toiletries | habit | decant to 100ml, or use hotel supply |
| Laptop for a leisure trip | habit | phone + notes |
| Beach towel | assumption | hotel towel, or quick-dry microfibre |
| Multiple books | comfort | one book + e-reader or phone |
| Umbrella + rain shell | belt and braces | one of them, weather-band dependent |
| Travel iron / steamer | formality | hotel service `[verify]` |
| Big first-aid kit | anxiety | 6 items that cover blisters, cuts, stomach, pain |

## Presenting the trades

Put the trade in the `Note` column, in one clause: `boots worn in transit, saves ~2L`. Never say
"space saving" without the direction: what was saved, and what it cost.
