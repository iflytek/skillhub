# Sample List - 6 days, city + one hiking day, carry-on only, mild spring

Worked example. Numbers are illustrative; the formulas are the point.

---

## 1. Packing Brief

```
Trip:        6 days / 5 nights, city base with one day trip
Weather:     mild 15-21C day, 9-12C night, 40% rain days  |  source: user-supplied range
Activities:  city sightseeing, one day hike (4h, marked trail), one business dinner
Luggage:     carry-on 40L, usable ~30L  |  laundry: sink, nightly
Travelers:   1 adult
Assumptions: sink laundry every 2 days, hotel provides towel and hairdryer,
             no medical or dietary constraint stated, trail gear not rented
Regulated:   2 item(s) flagged [verify] - see section 4
```

## 3. Wear-On-The-Day

- Walking shoes, trousers, base top, mid layer, shell in hand - saves roughly 8L of bag volume.
- Power bank and passport in the jacket, not the bag: both are needed at security anyway.
- The heavy mid layer is the single biggest volume saving on a mild trip; wearing it in transit
  is worth more than any packing cube.

## 4. Regulated & Restricted

| Item | Rule area | Authority to check | Risk if wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power bank | lithium battery carry-on rule | airline dangerous-goods page `[verify]` | confiscation at security, missed flight |
| Personal medication, 7 days | medication documentation | destination customs `[verify]` | seizure at the border |
| Sunscreen bought on arrival | liquid volume rule | airport security `[verify]` | discarding a new bottle |
| Razor | sharp object rule | airport security `[verify]` | confiscation, secondary screening |

Nothing in this list requires concealment. If a rule blocks an item, the answer is a different
item, a checked bag, or buying it at the destination.

## 5. Trim Plan

Volume check: must-items total roughly 26L against a 30L usable budget. The three cheapest cuts by
volume, in order:

1. Business trousers (2L) - only if the dinner is casual. Cost: one formal evening.
2. The 20L day pack (1L empty) - use the shell's stuff sack. Cost: nothing, mild discomfort.
3. Sleepwear (1L) - only if the hotel supplies robes `[verify]`. Cost: comfort, not function.

Cuts by weight: none needed. Batteries and shoes are the heavy items and both are `must`.

## 6. Notice

This is a planning list, not a purchase plan. Weather follows the band the traveller supplied, not
a live forecast. Every regulated row names who to verify with instead of asserting a limit.
